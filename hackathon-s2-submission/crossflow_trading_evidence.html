How paper execution works

Market and portfolio evidence enters Qwen. A structured LONG, REDUCE, CLOSE, or WAIT proposal passes deterministic freshness, sizing, exposure, loss, position, and autonomy checks. Approved Paper decisions execute automatically; blocked decisions are recorded without an order.
